# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-10-27

## Who We Are

Agent Compass is an open source career research plugin for Claude and Codex, maintained by Myo Win Thein. It helps IT and tech job seekers research countries for remote work or visa sponsorship, explore market salaries, find verified job portals and screen job descriptions against their profile. This policy covers the Agent Compass plugin and its documentation site, hosted on GitHub Pages.

## What Data We Collect

The documentation site has no account registration or data-entry forms. Its own code stores a theme preference in your browser and does not configure advertising or analytics tracking. Hosting and external-resource requests still involve the processing described below.

When you run `country-finder`, `salary-calculator` or `job-screener`, the assistant may use your resume, work history, citizenship, location, work rights and salary preferences. The plugin instructs the assistant to read and save profiles, research and workflow state in your selected workspace. These inputs are also handled by your selected AI service and any tools you enable. The plugin does not include a maintainer-operated server or telemetry endpoint that automatically receives your candidate files.

If you contact the maintainer by email or through GitHub, the maintainer receives the information you choose to send, such as your contact details, message and any attachments. GitHub issues are public; please keep private candidate information out of them.

## How We Use Your Data

The documentation site uses the `agent-compass-theme` local-storage value to remember your System, Light or Dark choice. The theme switcher reads this preference in your browser; it does not send the value to the maintainer. You can replace it by choosing another theme or remove it by clearing this site's browser storage.

The plugin uses the information available to your AI session to carry out the workflow you request, such as assessing country options, researching salaries or screening a job. Your provider, account type, settings and enabled tools determine how that information is processed outside the workspace.

Information you send directly to the maintainer is used to address your question, reported problem or privacy request.

## Data Sharing and Third Parties

The documentation site loads resources from the following services:

- **GitHub Pages:** hosts the site. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, including visits made without a GitHub account. See [GitHub's Pages data-collection information](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection) and [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **jsDelivr:** serves the Mermaid library used to display workflow diagrams. Loading it connects your browser to `cdn.jsdelivr.net` and its delivery providers. jsDelivr's policy describes processing request metadata such as IP address, browser information, requested resource and referring domain. See [jsDelivr's privacy policy](https://github.com/jsdelivr/jsdelivr/blob/master/Privacy%20Policy.md).
- **myowin.dev:** serves the site's favicon. Your browser makes a request to that host to load the icon, which exposes the IP address and request information sent by your browser to the serving host.

Your selected AI service and enabled tools separately process information used in a workflow. Review the terms, privacy notices and account controls that apply to your use of [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy) or [OpenAI](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/), including any business-account agreements. Research tools can also make requests to external websites.

Emails and GitHub messages are handled through the communication services you use. Links to job portals and other external sites take you to services with their own privacy practices.

## International Data Transfers

Hosting, content-delivery, email and AI services can process information in countries other than yours. Their locations, transfer arrangements and safeguards depend on the service and account you use; consult the applicable provider notices and agreements.

A workspace file's location does not determine where your AI service, hosting environment, backups or synchronization services process related information.

## Data Retention

Workflow files remain in your selected workspace until you manage or remove them using that workspace's tools. Chat history, uploaded files, backups and synchronized copies can have separate retention rules determined by your AI service and workspace setup.

The saved theme preference has no expiry configured by the site. It remains until it is replaced, browser storage is cleared or the browser removes it.

GitHub, jsDelivr, the favicon host and communication services control retention within their own systems. For information sent directly to the maintainer, use the private contact below to ask about retention or request deletion. Deleting a workspace file does not itself delete copies held in other services.

## Your Rights

Depending on the applicable law and circumstances, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability concerning personal information held about you.

For information you sent directly to the maintainer, contact the email below privately. For information held by your AI service, hosting provider or another service, use that provider's privacy controls or request channel. Manage files in your own workspace through the tools and permissions available to you.

## Children's Privacy

Agent Compass is intended for IT and tech job seekers and is not directed at children. Use of Claude or Codex is also subject to the applicable provider's eligibility requirements. If you believe a child has sent personal information directly to the maintainer, contact the private email below.

## Supervisory Authority

Where applicable law provides this right, you can lodge a complaint with the relevant data-protection authority. You can also contact the maintainer privately about information you have provided directly.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the plugin or documentation site after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

## Contact
