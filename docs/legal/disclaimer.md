# Disclaimer

**Last updated:** 2026-10-27

Agent Compass, maintained by Myo Win Thein, helps IT and tech job seekers research countries, salaries and job portals, and compare job descriptions with their profiles. Its output supports your own research and judgment. It is not professional legal, immigration or financial advice.

## AI-generated information

The AI model uses your supplied information and available research to produce results. It can misunderstand your profile, miss relevant facts, use outdated sources or present an incorrect conclusion confidently.

Citations, confidence ratings, audits and arithmetic checks help explain or check parts of a result. They do not guarantee that the underlying evidence is accurate, complete or current. Saved results reflect the inputs and evidence available when they were produced.

## Country, visa and salary findings

Country priorities and Remote or Sponsorship assessments help you plan where to research and apply. They do not guarantee suitable vacancies, employer interest, sponsorship, work rights or visa approval. A route's existence or an employer's permission to sponsor does not establish willingness to hire you. Confirm current requirements with the relevant official authorities before relying on them.

Salary Calculator estimates local-employee gross annual base pay for the researched role, level and country. The figures depend on the sources, geography, employment basis and adjustment assumptions recorded in the results. Monthly references use annual pay divided by 12; actual payment schedules can differ. A target is not an offer, and meeting a salary threshold does not establish overall visa eligibility.

## Portals and job screening

Portal verification reflects the pages and listings accessible on the stated date. Listings, access conditions and site ownership can change. Check the employer, vacancy and application route independently before sharing information or proceeding.

An Apply, Maybe or Skip recommendation reflects the job description and candidate information supplied. Missing or incorrect information can change the verdict. It does not predict a hiring decision, and the workflow does not automatically submit an application.

## Before acting

Review your profile facts, the assumptions, evidence dates and linked sources. Confirm important details before relocating, negotiating pay, accepting an offer or applying for a visa. Obtain qualified advice where your circumstances require it.

External websites and AI services have their own terms, availability and data practices.
