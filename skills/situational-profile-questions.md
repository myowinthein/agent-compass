# Situational Profile Questions

Check if situational-profile.md exists in the workspace. If it does, reuse it and skip the questions below. If it does not exist, ask me:

1. What is your current location?
2. What is your citizenship?
3. Is there any immigration friction, visa complexity, or employer risk perception generally tied to your citizenship? (or "none known")
4. What language(s) do you speak?
5. What language do you require for your work environment?
6. What is your minimum acceptable monthly salary, in what currency, and is it gross base pay or net/take-home? If you give one, is it a hard floor you won't go below, or context only (permission to use a recruiter-friendly local-market midpoint per country instead)? (or "not specified" to skip salary filtering)
7. In which countries do you currently live or explicitly hold work rights or immigration status that may matter to your search, even if you have not chosen targets yet? State the country and status (e.g. independent work rights, employer-tied visa, student visa), or "none" / "unknown". Citizenship or residence alone does not establish work rights.
8. What is your date of birth, or current age? Some visa salary thresholds vary by age. (or "prefer not to say" to skip age-based threshold checks)

Once answered, save these to situational-profile.md in the workspace. Date any supplied current age so it is not treated as timeless. Reuse existing profiles without adding required fields or re-asking the full intake; if a later comparison needs an unspecified salary basis or age/status reference date, ask only for that material clarification or report the check as unverified. Respect declined age disclosure.

Distinguish explicit none from unknown; a legacy "not applicable" answer is not proof of no rights anywhere. Apply the shared runtime's candidate-clarification rule for dated user-approved profile notes or disclosed run-only facts, including specialist handoffs. Do not silently edit existing answers.
