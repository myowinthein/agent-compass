# Situational Profile Questions

If situational-profile.md exists in the selected run, reuse it without restarting intake. Otherwise read the confirmed profile.md and this person's explicit answers in the selected run. Reuse applicable location, citizenship, languages, status and preferences; ask only for material missing or conflicting facts. Do not infer current facts from stale history, work rights from residence/citizenship, or acceptable working languages from spoken ability.

Use these questions only for unresolved context needed by the caller. Batch independent missing facts within QM limits rather than asking one at a time:

For Country Finder, location, citizenship, spoken/acceptable working languages and relevant work rights/status are core context. Ask once for missing core answers before proceeding; an omission is not an explicit unknown or declined answer. Accept an actual unknown/decline without repeating the question or inventing the fact.

1. Where are you currently based? Request a city/timezone only when needed for a location or timezone comparison.
2. What is your citizenship?
3. Which languages do you speak, and at what stated proficiency?
4. Which working language(s) do you require or accept?
5. In which countries do you explicitly hold work rights or immigration status relevant to this search? State the country and status, or "none" / "unknown". Do not re-ask current residence already established above.
6. Country Finder: do you have a minimum monthly pay you would not accept less than? This is optional; "not specified" skips floor filtering. Only for a supplied floor, collect its amount, currency and gross base versus net/take-home basis. If an existing or volunteered minimum is ambiguous, clarify hard floor versus context only before applying it; retain context-only answers without exclusions or asking permission to construct a local-market midpoint. Salary Calculator retains its minimum-salary question with amount/currency/pay basis and hard-floor versus context-only choice, including permission to use a recruiter-friendly local-market midpoint instead.

## Conditional context

Country Finder does not ask users to assess general citizenship-related immigration friction or employer risk perception. Research applicable restrictions and employer evidence. Ask about a personal circumstance only when a concrete research gap makes it relevant; a user-confirmed personal case fact differs from a general perception that needs verification. Preserve supplied concerns as research leads, not automatic penalties.

For Country Finder, age is not an intake prerequisite. Reuse a supplied dated age or birth date; otherwise ask only under skills/sponsorship-threshold-rules.md when an opened official rule shows a material age-dependent eligibility or salary-category difference. Explain the affected country/rule and request the least detail needed, such as age at the relevant event or an age band, rather than a full birth date. Only the parent asks; respect refusal and continue with the affected check unresolved. Do not ask again after declined disclosure unless the user reopens it.

Salary Calculator retains its additional intake questions about known citizenship-related immigration context and optional current age/date of birth. Preserve "none known" and declined age; do not turn reported general perceptions into verified restrictions. This caller's reference threshold research is unchanged.

## Save and reuse

Save established facts and supplied answers to situational-profile.md in the selected run, retaining their source/date context. A new file may include facts already confirmed in profile.md without another confirmation of unchanged facts. Optional Country Finder pay or age details left unspecified do not block discovery; record "not specified" or unknown, never a zero floor, invented age or implied disclosure refusal. Date any supplied current age. Reuse existing files without adding required fields; ask later only for a material unresolved fact or report the check as unverified.

Distinguish explicit none from unknown; a legacy "not applicable" answer is not proof of no rights anywhere. Apply the shared runtime's candidate-clarification rule for dated user-approved profile notes or disclosed run-only facts, including specialist handoffs. Do not silently edit existing answers.
