# Country Wealth Tiers — 2026

## Method

| Tier | Classification | GNI per person |
|---|---|---:|
| Tier 1 | Very high income | $30,000+ |
| Tier 2 | High income | $14,376–$29,999 |
| Tier 3 | Upper-middle income | $4,636–$14,375 |
| Tier 4 | Lower-middle income | $1,176–$4,635 |
| Tier 5 | Low income | $1,175 or below |

> The World Bank officially uses four income groups. Tier 1 and Tier 2 are a practical split of the official high-income group.
