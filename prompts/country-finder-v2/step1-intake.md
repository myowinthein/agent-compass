# Step 1: Candidate and search intake

Read `profile.md`. If `situational-profile.md` exists, read it too. Extract what is already known and ask only for missing or ambiguous items below. Present all required questions in one numbered message, then stop and wait.

1. One primary target role and seniority for this run.
2. Current country of residence and citizenship.
3. Date of birth or current age, because some visa salary thresholds vary by age.
4. Required working language and proficiency.
5. Current or former gross salary with currency and period, plus either:
   - a hard minimum, or
   - permission to use a recruiter-friendly local-market midpoint for each country.
6. Acceptable remote engagement types: direct employee, EOR, contractor, or any combination.
7. Maximum timezone difference, required overlap, or "no hard limit."
8. Relocation timeline, or "no deadline."
9. Whether dependant or family relocation support is required, preferred, or irrelevant.
10. Countries or regions to include and exclude. "Use default list" and "no exclusions" are valid explicit answers.

Accept explicit flexibility such as "no hard limit," "no deadline," "any arrangement," and "no preference." Do not accept vague claims such as "good salary" or "reasonable timezone" without a concrete interpretation.

If a historical salary is provided, explicitly record whether it is a hard floor or context only. Never silently convert it into a universal threshold.

Save the confirmed intake to `cfv2-step1-criteria.md` with these headings:

- Candidate
- Target role
- Remote track
- Sponsorship track
- Compensation posture
- Country scope

Tell the user the criteria were saved, update state to Step 1, and continue automatically to Step 2.
