Use only the audited Step 3 scores. Recalculate the priority formula once more and fix any arithmetic mismatch before writing the report.

Write `cfv2-step4-final-report.md` with:

1. **Bottom line:** strongest sponsorship markets, strongest remote-from-current-location markets, and strongest dual-track markets.
2. **Full ranked table:** every country, Remote score/class, Sponsorship score/class, Priority score, tier, confidence, and one-line reason.
3. **Two visible cutoffs:**
   - after Gold: custom-application or human-effort cutoff;
   - after Silver: broad active-monitoring cutoff.
4. **Effort allocation:** a practical distribution across core sponsorship, remote/dual-track, and vacancy-led markets.
5. **Balanced salary positioning for Gold countries:** local currency, period, and any legal-threshold caveat. Do not use historical salary as an international anchor.
6. **Portal/search strategy:** which countries to add, keep, reduce, or remove; require explicit current-location/APAC/worldwide evidence for remote roles.
7. **Changes from an earlier result:** include only when an earlier Country Finder output exists.
8. **Method and limitations:** scores are evidence-weighted decision aids, not statistical offer probabilities.
9. **Decisive source register:** direct links near the claims they support.

Keep remote and sponsorship visibly separate. Do not call a market "remote-friendly" when the evidence is domestic-only. Do not call a market "sponsor-friendly" solely because a visa exists.

In chat, show a concise result with the Gold countries, both cutoff locations, and the saved report path. Do not edit any vault or external profile automatically.

Update state to Step 4 and tell the user: "Country Finder v2 is complete. The reusable report is saved to cfv2-step4-final-report.md."
