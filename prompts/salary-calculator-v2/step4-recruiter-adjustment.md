# Step 4: Recruiter-Attraction Adjustment

Read `profile.md`, `scv2-step1-positioning.md`, `scv2-step3-audited-data.md`, and `skills/salary-calculator-v2-rules.md`. Use only the audited dataset and confirmed candidate facts.

For each complete country, choose one adjustment from `0%`, `3%`, `5%`, `7%`, `10%`, or `12%` under the v2 rules.

The adjustment must be tied to concrete, candidate-applicable employer cost, delay, uncertainty, sponsorship, relocation, onboarding, or interview friction. Never use race, ethnicity, passport prestige, abstract nationality ranking, or generalized assumptions about people from the candidate's country. Do not double-count a legal salary threshold as a discount factor.

For each country record:

- selected adjustment;
- confidence: High, Medium, or Low;
- concise evidence-backed reasons;
- factors considered but excluded and why; and
- whether the already-local/work-rights cap applies.

If evidence is insufficient, use `5%` with Low confidence. Never exceed the `12%` cap. Do not calculate final salaries in this step.

Save the output to `scv2-step4-adjustments.md` in confirmed country order. In chat, report only the completed count and saved path. Update the v2 state and continue automatically to Step 5.
