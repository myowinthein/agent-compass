# Step 1: Intake and Positioning

Read `profile.md` and `skills/salary-calculator-v2-rules.md` completely.

## Target role and level

Draft a short, role-specific career ladder using real title conventions for the candidate's field. Identify the candidate's likely current level and the target level that salary research should benchmark. Use the requested role, not the highest historical responsibility, and respect preferences such as individual-contributor rather than management-heavy work.

## Country scope

Look for Country Finder results in this order:

1. `cfv2-step4-final-report.md`
2. `cf-step6-final-ranking.md`
3. `cf-step5-scoring-results.md`

If a report exists, extract its Gold or active-application countries in the report's priority order. If the report has separate Remote and Sponsorship tracks, include a country when it is active on either track and record the applicable track or tracks. Show the ordered list to the user. Ask whether to use it as-is, add or remove countries, or start fresh. If no report exists, ask for the ordered target-country list.

## Missing facts

Reuse clearly answered facts from `profile.md` and `situational-profile.md`, but do not assume that an old salary figure is a hard floor. Ask only for missing or ambiguous items:

1. exact target role or roles and desired level;
2. current country of residence and citizenship;
3. current work authorization in any target country;
4. relocation, remote-from-current-country, or both for each country when the Country Finder track does not make this clear;
5. acceptable employment basis: employee, contractor, or both;
6. current or most recent base salary, its currency and pay period, and whether it is gross or net;
7. minimum acceptable salary, if any, explicitly labeled either **hard floor** or **context only**;
8. date of birth or age, because some visa thresholds vary by age;
9. required working language and any optional language preferences; and
10. any concrete, known immigration or relocation constraint. Do not ask for a passport ranking or subjective nationality prestige.

Do not accept vague salary units or an unclear hard-floor/context label. Once the facts are complete, show:

- the proposed target role and level;
- the ordered country list with employment track;
- the salary-floor interpretation; and
- the career ladder.

Wait for the user to confirm or correct this positioning. This is the only required checkpoint in this step.

After confirmation, save the authoritative intake to `scv2-step1-positioning.md`. Include the ordered countries and all confirmed facts needed by later steps. Update the v2 state and continue automatically to Step 2.
