# Step 3: Evidence Audit and Normalization

Read `scv2-step1-positioning.md`, `scv2-step2-salary-research.md`, every file under `scv2-research/`, and `skills/salary-calculator-v2-rules.md`. Stop if any required input is missing.

Audit every country independently. Reopen the decisive salary and immigration sources. A saved citation is not evidence until its page supports the associated claim.

For each country:

1. verify source identity, date, geography, role, level, currency, pay period, and compensation basis;
2. reject or downgrade inaccessible, circular, stale, mismatched, or unsupported evidence;
3. normalize only explicit, comparable data to annual gross base salary in local currency;
4. confirm that Market Low, Midpoint, and Strong describe one broad practical employer market, not mixed company tiers;
5. re-derive any market point affected by rejected or corrected evidence and show why;
6. verify the candidate-applicable sponsorship route and assign exactly one threshold state: Verified numeric, No fixed threshold, Not applicable, or Unknown;
7. detect a market/visa conflict when a verified threshold exceeds Market Strong; and
8. assign the final evidence grade.

If a critical claim can be repaired with additional current research, do that research now and record the new source. If the evidence remains too weak to support a number, mark the country incomplete instead of inventing one.

Save the authoritative audited dataset to `scv2-step3-audited-data.md` in the confirmed country order. Preserve both original and corrected values for every correction. In chat, report only verified count, incomplete count, and whether any market/visa conflicts were found. Update the v2 state and continue automatically to Step 4.
